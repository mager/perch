# Perch product context

## Register

product

## Users

Owners checking their own Mac mini or other authorized machines from a laptop or
phone. A quick glance should establish when the machine last reported and what
the latest snapshot actually says, including when the current state is unknown.

## Product Purpose

A private, self-hosted status portal that remains available independently of the
monitored Mac. Each installation uses its own accounts, storage, and credentials.
The first version reports heartbeats, resources, and tmux metadata. It does not
provide remote execution or infer task progress from foreground processes.

## Brand Personality

Precise, polished, friendly. The owner initially explored black terminal styling,
then explicitly chose an Apple-inspired white canvas, bold centered headings,
and generous space. The bird remains the identity. The owner enthusiastically
approved the generated bird perched on a silver Mini as the landing page's hero.

## Anti-references

Do not turn terminal-inspired styling into a command interface. Do not replace
accurate status with decorative activity or implied agent intelligence. Preserve
the readable, spacious hierarchy of the approved studies.

## Design Principles

- Put heartbeat freshness before resource values.
- Distinguish fresh, stale, unavailable, and sample data in words, not color alone.
- Make the personal tool feel welcoming through clear language and a bird mark.
- Keep the identity independently reusable and the implementation small.
- Treat the dashboard as a report, not a remote control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Phone-friendly layouts, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, text
status labels, and reduced-motion support. No hover-only information.
