# Perch assets

The owner selected the bird concept and then changed the initial dark terminal
exploration to a white, Apple-inspired presentation with centered bold typography.

- `public/logo.svg`: editable vector perched bird.
- `public/wordmark.svg`: custom path-based lowercase wordmark, no font dependency.
- `public/favicon.svg`: dark tile with a light bird for small browser icons.
- `public/perch-hero.png`: generated conceptual bird-on-Mini artwork, approved by
  the owner. Used on the landing page; not a photograph of a monitored machine.

The hero was generated with the built-in imagegen tool on October 4, 2026. The
original remains in the generating chat's generated_images directory. No API key
or external image service configuration was used.

## Hero prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Final hero artwork for Perch, an open-source private Mac
mini monitoring portal. A meticulously art-directed photoreal studio still life
on pure white: one compact unbranded silver aluminum mini desktop computer, very
clean rounded square enclosure, front viewed at low three-quarter angle, with a
tiny elegant sage-green songbird perched on its upper left edge. Bird natural and
calm, simplified sculptural feather detail, no cartoon face, no toy look. Computer
has two discrete front ports and one tiny subtle white indicator, NO Apple logo or
any other branding. Soft daylight and grounded natural contact shadow. Premium
understated industrial design, charming unexpected bird, exceptionally refined
surface texture, minimal composition. Center both objects together, occupy central
70 percent of wide landscape image, plenty of white around them. No text, no UI,
no decorative objects, no background room, no plants, no screens, no colored
gradient backdrop. Make the bird's silhouette distinctive and friendly. The work
should feel like a beautifully photographed small personal computer with an actual
tiny bird visiting. This is conceptual brand artwork, not an exact product photo.
